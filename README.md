One bowl of ramen, one ingredient doing all the talking: thick slices of
glossy, caramelized chashu pork, seared dark at the edges and glistening
in golden broth. Around them, only supporters — a jammy soft-boiled egg,
springy noodles, fresh scallions, a sheet of nori — in a dark ceramic
bowl, steam still rising, chopsticks waiting on the rim.

## How it works

`entry.png` is a 1024×1024 JPEG rendered with PIL. `main.ts` (Node 22,
standard library only, no network) copies it to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (falling back to `/output/entry.png`).
